# Independent review: Muja's research site (built `dist/`, 2026-11-02)

**Scope:** I tested the built site at `http://localhost:4321/mujaverse/` (69 sitemap URLs plus `/styleguide/` and `404.html`). I checked it against `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/*`, and checked facts against `docs/CONTENT-INVENTORY.md` and the owner's CV file in the repo root (`CV — Letter.pdf`, gitignored). Since that CV is in the repo, I used it to tell CV-sourced facts apart from invented ones.

**Tools:** curl, Playwright/Chromium, @axe-core/playwright (tags wcag2a/aa, 21a/aa, 22aa, best-practice) at 1280×900 and 360×800, gzip-9 sizes of the actual `dist/` files, and screenshots.

**What passed (so the findings below are in proportion):**
- axe: 0 violations on 22 of 23 pages at both widths.
- No horizontal scroll at 360 px.
- Exactly one `<h1>` per page and no heading-level skips.
- Skip link works.
- 0 KB of external JS on first load for every page tested.
- No YouTube request before the facade is clicked.
- With reduced motion, the 3D island does not mount and the poster stays.
- No `TODO` strings leak into `dist/`.
- No phone number or student ID in `dist/`.
- Every fact checked traces to the inventory or the CV. **I found no invented fact.**

However, the first screen at 1280×900 shows no research *question*, no figure and no result. The right half of the hero is empty.
