## Why it exists

In many places, people who question the powerful are silenced. Sound Society is a social website where anyone in a community can raise a problem — a broken road, a ruined hospital, a false promise — and ask the people responsible to answer, without giving their name. The whole community sees the question, and the office it concerns can answer in public.

There are deliberately no private messages, no followers and no blocking: the platform is for public questions, not for fights. Abusive or hateful posts are removed, and a report button sends false or harmful posts to the admins.

## How it is ordered

The “Most Urgent” tab ranks posts by `(votes + 1) / (hours since posting + 2)^1.2` — the same shape as Hacker News’ formula, with gentler gravity, so an important report in a small community does not sink too fast. The “Recent” tab shows the newest first.

## How it is built

- **Backend** — FastAPI with Pydantic, SQLAlchemy and Alembic on PostgreSQL; per-route rate limits; Cloudflare Turnstile against bots; email or Google sign-in.
- **Frontend** — React, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS.
- **Deployment** — one Docker image serving both, on free tiers (Render, Supabase for the database and media).
