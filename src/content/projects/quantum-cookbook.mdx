## Why it exists

The Quantum Cookbook is an interactive textbook on quantum computing, built as static HTML. It lives on its own site: [open the Quantum Cookbook](https://mujahidmahfuz.github.io/quantum-cookbook/).

## How it is built

Before any interactive widget is written, the numbers it shows are checked numerically (“verify-first”).
