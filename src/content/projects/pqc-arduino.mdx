## Why it exists

Post-quantum cryptography (PQC) is designed to stay secure against quantum computers, but many schemes need more memory than small microcontrollers have. This project tests which post-quantum signature schemes fit on an Arduino UNO, with 32 KB of Flash and 2 KB of SRAM.

<Explainer id="pqc-memory-budget" />

## What it covers

1. **Fit on the UNO** — SPHINCS+-SHA2-128s is the only viable scheme under these limits.
2. **Migration defects** — a taxonomy of defects in PQC migrations, mined from 8 GitHub repositories.
