## The problem

Studies how reliable retinal foundation models are for systemic-disease screening and risk estimation — transportability, calibration, uncertainty and explanation stability — with a Retinal Reliability Score and reason codes.

## Method

Reliability is examined along four axes: transportability across settings, calibration, uncertainty and the stability of explanations. These feed a Retinal Reliability Score that comes with reason codes. The models screen for and estimate risk; they do not diagnose.
