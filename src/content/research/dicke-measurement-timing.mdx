## The problem

Measuring the flag ancilla early — from gate 21 of 24, under the last-gate-targeting rule — leaves the successful output unchanged; the gate-cost saving is about 3% in the abstract cost model and about 1% with real two-qubit gate counts.

## Method

The study analyses where measurements can be moved earlier in Dicke-state expansion circuits without changing the result. The accepted paper finds gate 21 of 24 is the earliest safe point; the saving is about 3% in the abstract model and about 1% with real two-qubit gate counts.
